# Targeted manuscript-support selection rules

The primary statistics are frozen. Tables are read and summarized without refitting models. The top five recurrent measurements are ranked by number of globally BH-significant primary syndromes (q < .05 across 5,760 tests), with lexical feature-ID ties. Report all 48 tested syndromes, positive/negative counts, exact S6 formulas and anatomical groups. Regional rates are recomputed from all primary rows and asserted against the frozen regional summary, not ranked by raw discoveries alone.

Demographic examples are illustrative rather than additional inference. Analysis A uses the fixed readable features philtrum_length, inter_pupillary_distance and forehead_height in both layers, regardless of q. Analysis B requires at least 10 disease and 100 reference observations; rank syndrome/group cells by disease n, reference n, then lexical names. Take the first three distinct syndromes and assign those same three features in that order. Analysis C requires at least 10 disease and 100 reference observations in each group; rank cells by minimum disease n, minimum reference n, then lexical syndrome/group names; take the first three distinct syndromes and the same feature sequence. Select without looking at direction, magnitude or significance; report all selected effects, including nonsignificant examples. Effect magnitudes and uncertainty inform interpretation after selection. Full tables remain the source of record.

A is an omnibus reference-group test, not a syndrome effect. Report all group n/means/medians and omnibus H/q, without inventing group-pair uncertainty. B uses syndrome-minus-matched-reference Hedges' g, marginal bootstrap intervals and the global layer-B q. C uses (disease−reference in group 2)−(disease−reference in group 1), HC3 intervals in feature units and the global layer-C q. Correction families remain separate for each layer/analysis. Recorded groups are source annotations, not genetic ancestry or an intrinsic hierarchy.

Adjusted illustrations retain all seven prespecified models, with their four-model and three-model families separately labeled. Add the five pre-existing clinical anchors from the full 5,880-model screen, preserving its entire planned correction denominator. No new selected-model family is constructed. Clinical and novelty review evaluates all 17 appendix questions, including negative and unsupported cases; only a significant active association with directly related clinical trait support is retained as a supported clinical illustration. No source establishes the exact normalized ratio merely by describing a related trait.
