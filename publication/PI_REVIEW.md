# PI review before publication

PRISM is a statistical investigation using the pinned 120-feature FaceKit measurements. The main study and its demographic, within-GMDB, adjusted and robustness analyses use real GMDB and FairFace observations. PDIDB is a separate synthetic catalog and contributes no observations to these inferential families.

- Confirm the exact IRB protocol identifier, permitted study scope and mentor authorization for internal GMDB image reprocessing. The agent relied on the author's attestation and did not independently inspect the protocol.
- Real GMDB photographs, patient identifiers, landmarks and per-person measurements must not be published. Current manuscript figures are statistical plots, and the dashboard serves aggregate histogram bins rather than individual measurement arrays.
- Confirm source-specific FairFace attribution and image-reuse conditions before any photograph illustration. Dataset annotations do not establish clinical health, exact ages or biological ancestry. Current manuscript materials contain no photographs.
- Obtain PDIDB provider confirmation of generation lineage, patient-source linkage, independence and illustration redistribution terms. Gated access and synthetic designation do not establish image-publication permission; no PDIDB illustrations are included.
- Review authorship, funding, clinical wording and the final data/code release statement. The reported findings are exploratory geometric associations, with no held-out diagnostic-performance claim.
- Retired per-row datasets and metadata have been removed from the current checkout and preserved privately. Review their earlier repository-history exposure and remote copies. Archiving and current-file removal do not remove prior Git objects, clones or forks. History remediation and any additional release decisions require PI direction; no force push or history rewrite has been performed.

Concordance bootstrap intervals and uncertainty summaries are excluded because the assessed method was biased. Only numerically verified descriptive concordance points are manuscript-facing. Original excluded results are retained privately for audit.
