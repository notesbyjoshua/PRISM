# Historical 125-feature extension: analysis contract

This exploratory extension fixes its comparison rules before calculating the new results. This is an analysis contract, not an external preregistration. It reads the existing cohort selection and unadjusted outputs, the frozen FairFace screen, exact feature dictionary, and published reference correlation matrix/clusters. It never writes to those locations. New aggregate derivatives, reports, logs and plots stay in a new ignored directory; existing output directories cannot be reused.

## Full adjusted screen

The 49 historical target syndromes and 125 features define 6,125 planned hypotheses. Each target is compared with all other selected GMDB patients (including the 330 labels outside the 49), using `feature ~ intercept + target + (recorded age - 6) + female recorded label`. Complete cases are selected separately for each feature. Unknown sex labels are excluded; no imputation occurs. Effects are unstandardized feature-unit coefficients, with HC3 standard errors, normal-reference two-sided p-values and marginal 95% intervals.

Support requires at least 10 complete target and 20 other observations, at most 30% exclusion in either group, positive-width recorded-age range overlap containing at least 10 complete patients in each group, full design rank four, finite condition number below 1e8, positive residual variation, and maximum leverage below 1 - 1e-8. No observations are trimmed to the overlap interval. Range support is a minimal feasibility check, not covariate balance. Sparse age support, nonoverlapping central age ranges, sex cells below five and leverage above 0.2 are flagged. A shared linear age slope and recorded binary labels do not resolve other confounding. Unsupported models have no estimate/interval/q, retain a correction p-value of one, and remain in the 6,125-test BH denominator. Their reason is exported.

The pre-existing four-model analysis keeps its original four-model BH family and outputs. Its coefficients may reproduce, but its q-values must not be substituted for this full-screen correction. The new adjusted table is not Hedges' g and is not the unadjusted screen.

Regression tests a conditional linear mean coefficient; Mann-Whitney tests ranks/distributions. Differences in discovery counts across adjusted and unadjusted screens cannot isolate the impact of covariate adjustment. HC3 normal-reference inference remains approximate with small target groups. Correlated measurements and overlapping comparators do not establish the dependence conditions for nominal BH control; q-values are not a realized false-discovery fraction.

## Targeted pairwise comparisons

The four fixed anchors are Cornelia de Lange, Noonan, Williams-Beuren and Rubinstein-Taybi. Each is compared with every other member of the fixed historical 49-syndrome set. Duplicate unordered pairs are removed, leaving 186 pairs (six among anchors and 180 anchor-versus-other pairs), each with 125 features: one global 23,250-test BH family. Pair orientation follows anchor order, then sorted other label; a positive effect denotes the first group minus the second. Eligibility is at least 10 finite measurements in each group and at most 30% missingness in each; support is not assessed by the observed p-value. Nonestimable g (zero pooled variance) is flagged, with correction p=1.

Report Hedges' g with the historical correction `1 - 3/(4*(n_a+n_b)-9)`, a two-sided asymptotic Mann-Whitney test with tie and continuity correction, and marginal patient-row percentile bootstrap intervals (default 1,000 draws). Bootstrap each group independently, keeping all feature columns together. Failed/finite draw counts are exported; intervals with fewer than 95% finite draws are flagged. Pairwise comparisons are unadjusted exploratory anatomical contrasts, not diagnostic specificity, discrimination performance or clinical validation.

## Concordance extension

Reuse the existing all-other-GMDB and eligible-49 unadjusted screens and their original separate 6,125-test q-values. Compare each with the original frozen GMDB-FairFace screen; no new concordance significance tests or multiplicity families are invented. Report effect-direction agreement (including zeros), Pearson and Spearman correlations, significance overlap/Jaccard/retention, and attenuation as `abs(FairFace g) - abs(GMDB g)`, with positive values indicating a smaller absolute standardized effect under the GMDB comparator. This difference is descriptive; changes in standardization and comparator composition prevent a technical-bias or causal interpretation. Report overall, by anatomical region, and by the dictionary's 89 feature families.

Default 1,000 joint bootstrap replicates resample recorded GMDB patient rows within each of all retained syndrome labels, fixing syndrome sizes, and resample distinct FairFace paths. Use the same target rows and reference sample across all contrasts in each replicate; recompute both g matrices. This preserves shared observations and feature dependence. Reuse identical draws across the two comparator designs. Report marginal percentile intervals for continuous summaries and direction/attenuation fractions, not binomial intervals that treat 6,125 correlated entries as independent. Intervals condition on cohort/photo selection, syndrome composition and the recorded-ID assumption; they do not establish FairFace participant independence. There are no bootstrap significance-overlap intervals or recalculated q-values in this concordance bootstrap.

Export estimated bootstrap bias, SE, and a flag when an interval excludes its point estimate or absolute bias exceeds SE. This diagnostic was added after the first development run revealed displaced concordance distributions; the cohort, comparison families and numerical interval procedure did not change. Large-bias percentile intervals are conditional resampling summaries with unverified nominal population coverage, not validated confidence statements. Do not silently recenter or suppress them.

## Redundancy and age sensitivity

Reuse the frozen reference-only Spearman matrix and existing average-linkage clusters; do not refit correlations or redefine BH families. Report discovery concentration per cluster across the original FairFace, unadjusted within-GMDB and new adjusted screens, and absolute-rho threshold summaries. Reference dependence is descriptive and may differ in GMDB; cluster counts are not independent phenotype counts or an effective FDR denominator.

Age strata are fixed as [3,6) and [6,10). Within each, compare the original 49 targets with all other retained GMDB labels in that stratum. Keep 49 x 125 x 2 = 12,250 planned tests in a separate global BH family; at least 10 finite target and 20 comparator observations and <=30% feature missingness are required. Use the same g, rank-test and bootstrap procedure as pairwise comparisons. Export every sparse/unsupported cell. This is a new stratum-specific fit, unlike the previous B/C threshold re-correction. Adequate sample count does not establish statistical power. Sex is not adjusted in these exploratory strata. Differences in g or significance across ages are not interaction tests or longitudinal developmental effects.

## Reproduction and disclosure

Run `extended_analysis.py --output-dir NEW_IGNORED_DIRECTORY`, then the validation suite in the README. Defaults and seeds are saved before the fit. Only source, methods and tests are eligible for Git; all cohort derivatives require separate owner review. Recorded patients occur once and never on both sides of a contrast. Distinct recorded IDs cannot exclude relatives, duplicate biological persons or other hidden dependence. No authorization, measurements, image quality annotations or clinical validation are inferred.
