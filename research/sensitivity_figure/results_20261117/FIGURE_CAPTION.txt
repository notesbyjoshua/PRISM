Sensitivity of race-stratified FaceKit syndrome–feature associations. (A) Among 1,000 FairFace-only placebo iterations, 987 produced no globally BH-significant discoveries and 13 produced at least one. Only 2,760/9,600 planned hypotheses were supported; European placebo contrasts were unsupported because the reference sample exhausted the available pool. The mean was 0.021 discoveries per run (maximum 4). This same-source placebo does not test GMDB–FairFace source effects or establish a universally calibrated false-positive rate. (B) 100,000 permutations per null yielded 103 empirical point-estimate BH discoveries under category-only shuffling and 92 under category/sex/age-blocked shuffling, with 80 and 58 respective overlaps with primary findings in the same direction. 55 associations met cross-design point-estimate criteria; 0 met the conservative simultaneous Monte Carlo upper-bound criterion. Each null uses the planned 9,600-comparison correction family. The null designs are not independent replications or nested sets; their differing counts do not establish a causal effect of blocking. (C) Original versus larger-reference subset stability among the same original discoveries. African: original ref n=52, 76/135 (56.3%) stable; larger ref n=832, 112/135 (83.0%). Asian: original ref n=190, 193/252 (76.6%) stable; larger ref n=3040, 233/252 (92.5%). Combined: 269/387 (69.5%) versus 345/387 (89.1%). Each displayed design uses 1,000 draws. Stable means global BH q<0.05 in ≥80% of draws and original-effect g-sign agreement in ≥95%. The larger allocation is the largest paired ladder configuration that still varies reference selection in both categories. This configuration was selected by reference size, not stability outcomes. Both displayed designs use without-replacement subsets and fixed cases; global q is recalculated across all 9,600 tests in the source experiments. African/Asian sizes increase jointly, so global BH competition can contribute to q changes. Near-full subsets reduce finite-pool selection variation; this is not independent replication or a patient confidence interval. European reference photographs remain fixed at n=647 and are omitted from the stability bars. The deterministic full-pool diagnostic and its distinct with-replacement bootstrap are not plotted. These analyses evaluate internal sensitivity and establish neither independent clinical validation nor elimination of image-source confounding. Demographic labels are recorded annotations, not verified genetic ancestry.
