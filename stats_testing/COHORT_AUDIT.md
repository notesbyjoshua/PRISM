# Current FaceKit cohort audit

Run `.venv/bin/python stats_testing/cohort_audit.py --dry-run` to verify the current schema, finite vectors, quality flags, unique recorded GMDB patient IDs and unique FairFace paths. Current: 1,845 selected patients, 3,409 reference paths and 48 primary syndrome cohorts. PDIDB is excluded. Reference person identity and clinical health are unverified. Earlier research outputs and source are private read-only archives.
